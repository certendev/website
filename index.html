Live Rail Crossing Monitor

Challenge: Blocked rail crossings delayed emergency response, and crews had no live way to know which routes were clear.

Solution: A real-time status board that pushes crossing updates instantly, showing train direction and estimated clear times, and clearly flagging any crossing whose data can't be verified.

Built for every screen: Fully responsive. View on a phone in transit or on a TV screen in the station. The content automatically sizes to the display for maximum visibility.

Uniform Ordering App

Challenge: Uniform requests came in on paper forms and email, then were logged in Excel, making it hard to track sizes, approvals, and spending.

Solution: A catalog-style ordering app with photos and specs for every item and employee type. Sizing fields adapt to each item, so crews only see what applies.

  • Approval workflow routes each order to the right supervisor
  • Automated reminders when orders are ready for pickup
  • Pickup audit trail confirms who received what, and when

Records Compliance with Microsoft Purview

Challenge: Sensitive patient and personnel records were stored without consistent protection, and public records requests meant days of manual searching.

Solution: A sensitivity labeling and retention framework aligned to state records schedules, with data loss prevention to stop protected health information from being shared.

  • DLP blocks patient data in Teams and email before it's sent
  • Sensitivity labels with automatic labeling for PHI
  • Retention policies matched to state records schedules
  • Faster public records searches with eDiscovery
